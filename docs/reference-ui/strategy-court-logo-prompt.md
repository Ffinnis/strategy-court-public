## Final prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original logo concept for Strategy Court, a trading strategy research and historical stress-testing application. The raster is a visual concept to be redrawn by hand as a clean SVG for a website header and favicon.
Primary request: design one compact, distinctive geometric symbol. Two broad, interlocking stepped ribbons should suggest an S and an open C through negative space, with a quiet reference to a price moving through a testing boundary. Prioritize a memorable silhouette and balanced negative space over literal illustration.
Scene/backdrop: solid near-black #080808, generous empty margin.
Style/medium: crisp, flat, vector-like brand mark, precise orthogonal geometry with very slightly softened corners. One centered white symbol, no text.
Composition/framing: square canvas, logo occupies about half the width, optically centered. Shape must still read at 24 pixels.
Color palette: white on black only.
Constraints: original design; 2 or 3 simple filled shapes; clear open counters; no outline badge surrounding the mark; no shadows or 3D on the mark; no gradients; no gavel, scales of justice, shield, checkmark, bull, coin, rocket, sparkle, or generic rising arrow; no wordmark, annotations, mockup, or watermark.
```

## Manual SVG interpretation

The concept suggested an interlocking S with open C-shaped counters and stepped cuts. The production SVG was drawn manually with two filled paths. Removed the generated glow, reduced the number of interior steps, widened the counters and normalized the proportions for a small header mark. No automatic bitmap tracing or embedded raster is used.

The header wordmark remains live text in the application's primary sans-serif typeface. The favicon uses the same two paths on a dark background.
